Forge your legend in Viking Saga

An idle RPG for Android. Forge gear, fight monster waves through six eras from the Viking north to a crystal age, hatch flying pets and tame mounts. Your hero keeps fighting while you're away.

What awaits

ForgeHammer out gear with runes and level your forge for rarer drops, from Common to Crystalline.
Six erasBattle waves and chapter bosses from Viking Saga through Dragon Kingdoms, the Sky Seas and beyond.
Pets and mountsHatch dragons, gryphons and birds in the Pet Aerie and tame mounts in the Beast Den.
Dungeons and towerBeat guardians for gold, runes, eggs and shards, then climb the Tower of Heroes.
MasteryRaise lasting skills one random level at a time.
RankingsCompare your power and tower floor with everyone on Google Play.
