Delete Your Account and Data

Viking Saga's Idle RPG by TrickyShadow · App package com.trickyshadow.vikingsaga

In short

  • Your game account is the chat and friends account on our server. Delete it in the game in a few taps, or email us and we delete it for you.
  • You can also ask us to delete just your chat messages and keep your account.
  • Everything on our server about you is deleted. Nothing is kept, apart from server backups, which are erased within 30 days.

Delete it in the game

  1. Open the game and tap the chat bar just above the tabs at the bottom of the Battle screen.
  2. Open the Friends tab.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete my chat account, then Delete.

Your account is deleted right away. Your game progress on the phone is not touched; opening the chat again makes a new, empty account.

Or ask us to delete it

No longer have the game? Email us from any address with the name of your hero (and your friend code, if you know it). We delete the account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

Email a deletion request

support@playvikingsaga.com

Delete only your chat messages

Want to keep your account and friends but remove what you wrote? Email us with the name of your hero and ask us to delete your chat messages. We delete all of them within 30 days and reply to confirm.

Email a request to delete my messages

What is deleted

  • your account: Play Games player ID, hero name, profile picture, power and stage,
  • your hero card (the gear, mount and stats other players could see) and the resource readings your game reported,
  • all your chat messages, friends and friend requests, the players you blocked,
  • reports you sent or that were made about you, moderation notes about you, and any server mail (gifts or corrections),
  • the sign-in keys for your phones.

Kept: nothing. Copies of the server database made before updates are kept for at most 30 days and then erased, so your data is gone from those within 30 days too.

Data we don't hold

  • Game progress on your phone: removed when you uninstall the game.
  • Google Play cloud save: stored in your Google account. Delete it in the game with Profile → Settings → Cloud save → Unlink & delete, or in the Google Play Games app.
  • Purchases and ads: handled by Google; see the Google Privacy Policy.
